PIRITORI → EDEN

Kallio, seven compressed days. A fiction.

The city already moves. People go to work, to school, to the shop, and home again — and they are the only cover you have.

Draw the lines they ride. Then put something else on the same lines, at the same capacity, and hope it lands while the price is still good.

Drag stop to stop to draw a line. Tap a stop to trade. Heat belongs to the line, not to you — watch which one starts to have a shape.
