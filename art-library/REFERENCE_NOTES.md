# Art reference notes

These sources ground location silhouettes and period cues. They are references,
not texture or illustration assets to copy into the game.

## Karhupuisto

- The City of Helsinki describes Karhupuisto as a small Kallio park named after
  Jussi Mäntynen's *Bear on the Anthill* sculpture:
  <https://tpr.hel.fi/TPRalusta/en/notification/info/2784/>
- HAM identifies the landmark as a red-granite sculpture unveiled in 1931:
  <https://www.hamhelsinki.fi/en/sculptures/mesikammen-muurahaispesalla-the-bear-on-the-anthill/>

The library uses an abstract bear-shaped red-granite prop rather than an exact
reproduction. The location plate leaves the sculpture modular.

## Sörnäinen harbour

- A 1999 aerial photograph records the active harbour's paved yards,
  warehouses, cranes, containers, trailers and vessels:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:S%C3%B6rn%C3%A4inen_Harbour_in_Helsinki,_Finland,_1999_-_2.jpg>

The Era I plate rejects the later Kalasatama skyline and keeps the broad yard,
warehouse, crane and quay silhouettes.

## Kallio fabric

- The City of Helsinki's Torkkelinmäki history provides local architectural and
  historical orientation around the Kallio and Karhupuisto area:
  <https://historia.hel.fi/fi/alueet/keskinen/torkkelinmaki>
- A June 2004 photograph of Pengerkatu shows a quiet Kallio street framed by
  pale multi-storey apartment blocks with repeated rectangular windows, simple
  façades, railings, parked cars and scattered trees:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pengerkatu.jpg>
- The Helsinki City Museum record for Porthaninkatu 12 and 14 grounds the mix of
  cobbles, parked cars, red brick, leafless park trees and the Kallio Library
  and church silhouettes:
  <https://www.finna.fi/Record/hkm.HKMS000005%3Akm002jom>
- Aleksis Kiven katu 40 is a protected 1909 apartment building. A documented
  courtyard renovation shows one coherent U-shaped plaster building around a
  paved yard rather than unrelated façade styles:
  <https://www.hekaoy.fi/en/kohde/aleksis-kiven-katu-40>
- The City Museum's Picture Browsery provides the broader historical photo
  collection used for street and courtyard checks:
  <https://www.helsinginkaupunginmuseo.fi/en/picture-browsery/>

The replacement courtyard uses generic Kallio plaster or brick, a granite base,
repeated window bays, courtyard railings and drainpipes rather than reproducing
a private courtyard. All visible wings must belong to one coherent perimeter
block. The original alley plate is retained only as a `needs-rework` record.
