PIRITORI
to EDEN

Chapter one — the square, 2003. Thirty evenings.

Igor fronted you two thousand. The square takes everyone eventually; the book your mother left says you may choose otherwise. Sinä saat.

Tap a station to travel — every journey costs the evening. Buy low where the lines are quiet, sell dear where they cross, and watch the nine o'clock news: what Arvi reads tonight moves the street tomorrow.

Every person and every bulletin in this story is invented. The harm the trade does is not — this is a story about that too.
