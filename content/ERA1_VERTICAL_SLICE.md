# Era I vertical slice — seven-day spine

Status: implementation baseline. Balance remains tunable.

## What the slice proves

The slice is not a short Drug Wars table followed by a separate tactics demo.
It proves one connected loop:

> observe the ordinary city → enter a person-led encounter → learn or alter an
> offer → assign a route and crew → resolve a mission or short formation battle
> → see the result return to the map, ledger and family.

Every one of the five UX modes is used:

| Mode | Slice proof |
|---|---|
| Route | eight active public anchors, ordinary flow, crew routes, local pressure |
| Encounter | Piritori, Jaska, bank, Toko, park, McCormicks, restaurant front |
| Ledger | one product, five offers, debt, dead markka, crew and equipment |
| Battle | information-avoidable 2v2 in Karhupuisto; 3v3 in a courtyard |
| News | scheduled Arvo bulletin with documented, inference and fiction fields |

## Seven-day rhythm

| Day | Day | Night | Capability shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Piritori first purchase | Jaska's receipt collage | money, information and remembered behaviour |
| 2 | first Siltasaari sale | Mira at the tram stop | a route becomes price history; first crew option |
| 3 | staffed bank after Arvo's markka bulletin | Toko lowers his voice | dead money becomes a placed decision; ranges and sabotage open |
| 4 | Samira reads Karhupuisto | McCormick family yard | intent readability, muscle and close equipment |
| 5 | avoid or resolve the 2v2 | first firearm offer at Piritori | battle consequence without mandatory gun ownership |
| 6 | restaurant-front introduction | courtyard 3v3 or deliberate refusal | faction access, critical risk and family proximity collide |
| 7 | debt, treatment and Pasila ledger | Jaska's unfinished map | Pasila becomes nearer, deferred, expensive or haunted |

Refusing the first purchase changes relationship and information order but
cannot create an unwinnable state. Refusing the firearm also remains viable.

## Battle test

Both battles use mirrored 3×3 half-boards beneath an isometric scene. Only
occupied, selected, targeted and reachable cells are normally visible.

- The Karhupuisto 2v2 teaches cover, intent, withdrawal and the value of Toko's
  information. Death is not an outcome.
- The courtyard 3v3 tests front/middle/back positioning, screening, marking,
  negotiation and critical wounds. A death is possible only when a clearly
  flagged critical wound remains untreated at final settlement.
- Aatami commands and appears in surrounding narrative; he is not a regular
  field unit.

## Narrative and historical boundary

All named criminal exchanges and teams are fictional. The Arvo markka bulletin
uses the Bank of Finland and ECB only for public facts: markka was no longer
legal tender, the conversion rate was fixed, and redemption continued. The
specific staffed counter and its gameplay consequences are fiction.

No content includes dosage, concealment, procurement procedure, weapon handling
or police-evasion instruction. Adults in ordinary public space remain people,
not scenery or enemy types.
