## Source-status grammar

- **DOCUMENTED** — directly supported by the linked report.
- **INFERENCE** — a plausible systemic connection drawn from multiple sources.
- **ACCUSATION** — a character’s contested causal or political claim.
- **FICTION** — invented character, organisation, route, choice or outcome.

Arvo Linde can use these labels on full news cards in later slices. The baseline
communicates the distinction in prose and this ledger.
