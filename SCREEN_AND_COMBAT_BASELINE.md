# Screen and combat baseline

Owner direction, 2026-08-17. This supersedes the earlier one-screen/no-combat
assumption.

`GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md` now supersedes this short note for interaction-mode
scope, 3v3 rules, encounter design and vertical-slice content. Keep this file as
the record of the owner correction that introduced separate maps, locations,
markets and battles. Its art notes remain a historical record; `ART_BIBLE.md` now owns visual and
asset-production decisions.

## Screen architecture

### 1. Operations map

One full screen for Kallio routes, territory, ordinary movement, crew pawns,
active missions and a compact menu. Selecting a token opens only a small
attached mission tab. No market table, news article or full inventory lives on
the map.

### 2. News and events

Arvo Linde broadcasts, historical texture, family scenes, faction consequences
and explicit source-status labels. Choices return a change to the map, roster or
market. In Era I, this is visibly a television-led screen: major reports arrive
through a scheduled broadcast, SMS carries short personal or operational
messages, and online information requires a separate desktop terminal.

### 3. Market, crew and inventory

The Drug Wars layer: district prices, debt, stock and time. The same screen owns
recruitment, weapons, armour and loadout comparison. Its available locations
expand through map missions.

### 4. Tactical battle

A separate alternating-turn encounter. The player deploys a small recruited
team; the team acts, then the opposition acts. Weapons, armour, injuries,
position/status and casualty risk matter. Aatami issues the mission and is not a
combat unit.

## First-generation progression

1. Buy a small amphetamine quantity at Piritori.
2. Send a tiny crew through nearby Kallio streets to sell it.
3. Encounter rivals and complete small movement or intimidation missions.
4. Unlock Toko Slomo’s information and sabotage gamble.
5. Recruit cheap operatives at Piritori.
6. Buy the first firearm at Piritori, unlocking tactical encounters.
7. Reach the McCormicks at Siltanen for hired guns, weapons and sabotage.
8. Contest restaurant nodes controlled by the Jade Lantern Network.
9. Expand the price map and wholesale market as territory and intelligence grow.
10. Transition Aatami from street operator to commander of a replaceable but
    narratively meaningful roster.

## Historical art direction that led to Art Bible v1

- full-screen map based on recognisable Kallio street relationships;
- shallow handmade collage districts, roads, buildings and interface panels;
- actual printed carton, packaging and newsprint clippings construct forms rather
  than merely decorating a cardboard-textured background;
- clean solid cardstock carries essential UI information, while irregular found
  fragments carry characters, places and narrative texture;
- some edges are cleanly cut and others ripped, folded or corrugated;
- small physical shadows, lifted corners and overlaps reveal the 2D layers;
- avoid the thick, bevelled or embossed look of carved cardboard props;
- cyan player routes, pink goods flow and restrained orange hostility;
- no news, market or combat tableau crowded into the map screen;
- faction icons must be specific invented marks, never ethnic caricatures.

### Historical broadcast exception, retained in Art Bible v1

- Arvo Linde may be a reusable stylised 3D presenter produced in Meshy or an
  equivalent character pipeline for lip sync, idle motion and restrained hand
  gestures;
- only the moving image inside the television uses 3D; the CRT shell, screen UI
  and surrounding world remain physical collage;
- posterisation, limited colour, analogue softness and subtle scanlines bind the
  3D render into the early-2000s television layer;
- the model should be animation-ready and recognisable at small size, not a
  photoreal likeness of Arvi Lind.

## Era I interface lock

- primary currency: euro (€);
- old markka may appear as a separate, unusable balance until exchanged;
- teller exchange is a physical bank action with time and exposure costs;
- the fixed historical conversion is 5.94573 markka to one euro;
- television, feature-phone SMS and desktop internet are separate interfaces;
- no smartphone shell, app grid, push notification or touch-first metaphor;
- material language: clean stacked carton, selective torn fibres and soft
  physical shadows, with texture supporting rather than obscuring information.
