    Fighter module

    Jade is your crew. Rust is the opposition. Each team has F01 Heavy and F02 Wiry, with matching loadouts.

    Each fighter gets one action per round: attack, reposition, brace or use their single bandage. A move spends the action. Cover blocks weapon lanes. Green cells show valid repositioning; targets show whether an attack reaches.

    Tap a crew card, choose an action, then tap the board or use the visible target/cell buttons. Drag the board to orbit. Camera buttons and mouse wheel zoom. FIT restores the wider full-board overview. J1/J2 identify Jade; R1/R2 identify Rust. Tags show condition and G for guard; full names appear in crew and target cards. Orientation changes briefly pause presentation while the view fits. Keyboard: 1/2 select, A/M/B/I actions, Enter ends round, Escape closes choices. Controller: D-pad moves focus, A activates, B cancels; triggers zoom.

    Animation status: repaired v05 models use their own idle/walk clips. Attacks, brace, item, hit, down and retreat are prototype gestures. Action rehearsal shows every gesture on all four actors and then resets the battle.

    The night courtyard studies the mood and lighting of Piritori’s existing 2D backgrounds. Environment geometry is a first pass. This module has no campaign costs, police escalation or final animation approval. C.04 lost graphics on the owner’s phone. C.05 starts touch devices at 30 FPS with smaller textures, capped resolution and no shadow pass; it restores the current fight after a graphics reset when the browser allows it.
