    Graphics

    Night Shift renders directly without the full-screen smoothing pass. Safe graphics also skips planar reflection rendering; it keeps the same crew, board and rules.

    Choose crew → rescue or recover → extract → deal with the aftermath.

    C.19 adds focused labels and objective guidance to the unified interface and direct scene rendering, retaining the conservative mobile WebGL startup recovery from C.17.1. Character production remains out of the critical path. The figures are deterministic procedural development bodies with individual silhouettes and accessories, not production character approvals or external model replacements.

    Jade is your crew; Rust is the opposition. Each fighter has one Move (up to four orthogonal steps) and one Action, in either order. Reload spends only Action. Blue full cover blocks movement and sight; gold low-wall edges reduce gun accuracy by 25 percentage points only across the wall. Flanks stay exposed. Either side can use a wall; movement routes go around it. Fighters crouch beside it and rise to shoot. Rust commits its targets and routes at the start of each round. Broken plans cancel; they never silently retarget. Combined danger assumes every shot hits. Tap a fighter or roster card, choose an action, then preview a cell or target and confirm. A knife bypasses one guard; other damage hits guard first. For a handgun, choose a target then Aim view. Inspect the same odds and cover before Confirm attack; Overview or Cancel returns without spending anything. The optional FOCUS toggle also directs fired shots. VIEW opens Tactical, Oblique and Overhead cameras. Drag to orbit; FIT restores the tactical overview. Crew opens the roster. Keyboard 1–6 selects crew, A/M/B/I/R actions, Enter ends the round. Controller D-pad focuses controls, A activates, B cancels.

    Help raises an adjacent fallen ally once. Extract spends Action at any south-edge cell. Boots allow a second Move by spending Action; a field kit can treat one adjacent ally. Normal Night Shift keeps its separate browser roster. Open with ?campaign=1 to project the campaign’s chosen crew into this outing: supported authored weapons, perks and skills cross the seam; supplied test gear stays marked as prototype. Aftermath writes new wounds, recovery or missing state back exactly once and never heals an injury that existed before the outing.

    The 2/6/12 participant fixtures exercise the C tactical resolver. They are capacity tests without mission or campaign costs. Changing fixture starts a fresh test.

    Wet paving uses a small authored lighting probe and variable surface roughness. It does not reflect moving characters. Scenery cuts away in front of the figures without changing cover. Physical Pixel 10 Pro and iPad M2 acceptance is pending.
