    Night Shift · field notes

    Choose crew → rescue or recover → extract → deal with the aftermath.

    Development stand-ins keep the environment playable while the approved fighters undergo rig and motion repair. These are neutral test bodies, not new character designs.

    Jade is your crew; Rust is the opposition. Each fighter has one Move (up to four orthogonal steps) and one Action, in either order. Reload spends only Action. Blue full cover blocks movement and sight; gold low-wall edges reduce gun accuracy by 25 percentage points only across the wall. Flanks stay exposed. Either side can use a wall; movement routes go around it. Fighters crouch beside it and rise to shoot. Rust commits its targets and routes at the start of each round. Broken plans cancel; they never silently retarget. Combined danger assumes every shot hits. Tap a fighter or roster card, choose an action, then preview a cell or target and confirm. A knife bypasses one guard; other damage hits guard first. Drag to orbit; FIT restores the overview. Keyboard 1–6 selects crew, A/M/B/I/R actions, Enter ends the round. Controller D-pad focuses controls, A activates, B cancels.

    Help raises an adjacent fallen ally once. Extract spends Action at any south-edge cell. Boots allow a second Move by spending Action; a field kit can treat one adjacent ally. Wounds, missing crew and returned equipment persist between outings on this browser. This separate pilot does not change your chapter campaign.

    The 2/6/12 participant fixtures exercise the C.11 tactical resolver. They are capacity tests without mission or campaign costs. Changing fixture starts a fresh test.

    Wet paving uses a small authored lighting probe and variable surface roughness. It does not reflect moving characters. Scenery cuts away in front of the figures without changing cover. Physical Pixel 10 Pro and iPad M2 acceptance is pending.
