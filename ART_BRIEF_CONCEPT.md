# PIRITORI → EDEN — concept art brief

A condensed art direction for an outside illustrator or image model. Distilled
from `BRIEF.md` (owner-authored) plus the 2003/2024 narrative direction. Where
this and `BRIEF.md` disagree, `BRIEF.md` wins.

---

## 1. What the game is, in one breath

A Helsinki city-flow and market game. You see the ordinary city moving —
people commuting, studying, shopping, going home — and underneath it a second
network carrying something else along **the same routes, the same trams and the
same limited capacity**. Draw the lines. Move product on them. Reach a way out
called Eden before the network owns you.

Two generations: **2003 Kallio** (Aatami) and **2024 Pasila** (his sons).

**The single most important visual idea:** the legal city and the illegal city
are *the same city*. Not a neon underworld beneath a grey one — one printed
map, where a second thing is moving that you have to learn to see.

---

## 1b. Use a real map of Kallio

**Owner direction: work from real Helsinki geography, not an invented layout.**

Open an actual street map of Kallio and trace from it. The coastline at
Hakaniemenranta, the bend of Hämeentie at Kurvi, the grid of the linjat, the
hill the church stands on, the block Karhupuisto sits in, Vaasankatu running
down to Hämeentie. Those relationships should survive into the diagram even
after it is compressed and stylised for play.

The in-game diagram is deliberately simplified — eight stops, straight runs —
but **the concept art should read as this specific district to somebody who has
walked it**, not as a generic transit poster. If a Helsinki player cannot point
at the church, the bay and the Kurvi bend, the drawing has missed.

Useful reference terms: *Kallio*, *Vaasanaukio / Piritori*, *Hämeentie Kurvi*,
*Kallion kirkko*, *Karhupuisto*, *Hakaniemen halli*, *Sörnäinen*, *Vaasankatu*,
*Kuudes linja*. Period reference for act one is early-2000s; act two present day.

---

## 2. The house look — non-negotiable

**A living printed civic map. Not a satellite view, not a game HUD, not a 3D
city.**

- **Risograph / photocopy.** Paper grain, screen-print flats, slight
  registration drift (colour layers a hair out of alignment), municipal
  typography. It should look *duplicated*, like something handed out at a
  transport office and then written on.
- **Flat colour.** Screen-print separations, not gradients or rendering.
- **People are tiny marks** — ticks, dashes, dots. Never characters, never
  faces, never icons of little people.
- **The map gets overwritten as it grows** — taped, stamped, corrected,
  annotated in pen. Not brighter. *Dirtier and more used.*
- Must stay readable **on a phone held in one hand**.

### Two skins, one geometry

| | **Piritori → Eden** (night) | **Toko Move** (day) |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | charcoal, dirty off-white | warm off-white day paper |
| Water | cold blue | pale sky blue |
| Accents | magenta = the product/player · **orange = immediate pressure only** | mint, sky, coral, yellow |
| Type | dirty off-white | clean dark |
| Mood | deadpan, intimate, funny, bleak | warm, readable, busy, optimistic |

The same street geometry must be recognisable in both. That contrast *is* the
brand of the pair.

### Palette

```
NIGHT   paper #0f1216 · ink #e2dccd · dim #8c8778 · water #1b2c3a
        orange #ff7a1a (pressure only) · magenta #F0027F (product/player only)
DAY     paper #f4f1e8 · ink #20272e · water #bcd8e6
        mint #5aa860 · sky #2f9fb8 · coral #e2683c · yellow #e0a53a
```

## 3. Do NOT draw

- **Mini Metro's language** — no coloured geometric station symbols (circle /
  triangle / square stations), no passenger-shape icons, no zen-clean vector
  calm.
- **Mini Motorways' language** — no coloured houses, no map pins, no road tiles.
- **Cyberpunk.** The shadow layer is *discoverable beneath* the city — a second
  ink pass you notice — never a glowing overlay.
- **Crime-movie iconography** — no Scarface, no cash fans, no gold chains, no
  weed leaves, no gang caricature, no police as faceless riot-armour villains.
- **Drug paraphernalia as decoration.** Use is neither sanitised nor sold as
  lifestyle.
- No real, identifiable people or their likenesses. Every character is invented.
- No photographic realism anywhere.

## 4. The world

**2003 — Kallio.** Working-class central Helsinki. Real districts:
Vaasanaukio (street name: **Piritori**), Kurvi, Vaasankatu, Kuudes linja,
Kallion kirkko (the grey church tower), Karhupuisto (the bear statue),
Hakaniemi (market hall), Sörnäinen (metro). Trams, wet asphalt, neon bar signs,
euros still new in people's pockets.

**2024 — Pasila.** Rail yards, the Tripla mall, tower blocks, a big police
station, drones and armoured vans. Colder, harder-edged, more surveilled.

**Cast** (all fictional):
- **Aatami Taskila** — 26, one jacket, one debt. Act one.
- **Jaska** — his brother. Paints. Charcoal on his hands. Karhupuisto bench.
- **The McCormicks** — a large Irish bar family on Kuudes linja. Muscle.
- **Toko Slomo** — Japanese noodle chef, Vaasankatu. Steam on the window.
- **Igor** — holds the paper on Aatami. Sörnäinen back booth.
- **Kalle & Aaro** — Aatami's sons, act two. Cain and Abel: one takes the
  business, one is a musician who leaves.

**Eden** — per `BRIEF.md`, not another profitable district but *the life the
player believes this money will buy, deliberately kept just beyond the edge of
the map.* Its final meaning is an open owner decision; do not illustrate it as
a specific place until it is locked.

## 5. Shot list — what to actually make

Ranked. **1–3 matter most.**

**1. The map, night.** The hero image. Kallio as a printed transit diagram on
charcoal paper: coastline, rail, district blocks, eight stops, two or three
fat drawn lines, tiny pale marks queuing at stops. Somewhere in it, a *second*
faint magenta trace riding the same lines. Registration drift. Grain.

**2. The same map, day.** Identical geometry, Toko Move skin. Warm paper, mint
and sky lines, the same tiny marks, a soft stain of pollution behind a busy
route. Proof the two are one city.

**3. One stop, close.** A single node blown up: the disc, its glyph, a queue of
marks growing past the shelter's capacity, an orange warning ring starting.
Shows how load is read at a glance.

**4. Heat staining a line.** One route on the night map going from clean to
*noticed* — the ink thickening, a hand-stamped mark, an annotation. No wanted
bar, no meter: the stain is on the street, not on the player.

**5. Key art / title.** `PIRITORI → EDEN` in municipal type over the night map,
with somewhere at the very edge of the sheet half cropped off by the paper's
border — Eden is what the map does not reach.

**6. Act two, 2024.** Pasila and Tripla in the same printed language — colder
palette, more infrastructure, a drone mark in the sky, the diagram now denser
and more corrected.

**7. Character marks** (low priority). If characters appear at all, they are
**map annotations** — a hand-drawn ring around a stop with a name beside it,
not portraits.

## 6. Prompt seeds

Paste and adjust. Keep "risograph", "flat", "registration drift" and "no
gradients" in every one.

> **Night map —** Risograph-printed transit diagram of a compressed Helsinki
> district on dark charcoal paper. Flat screen-print colour separations, visible
> paper grain, slight registration drift between layers, municipal sans-serif
> labels. Off-white and cold blue route lines, tiny pale tick-marks queuing at
> circular stops, one faint magenta trace running along the same lines. Hand
> annotations and a rubber stamp in one corner. No gradients, no 3D, no glow,
> no photographic detail. Flat, printed, used.

> **Day map —** The same transit diagram, printed on warm off-white day paper.
> Mint, sky-blue, coral and yellow flat inks, clean dark type, tiny dark
> tick-marks moving along the routes, a soft pale stain spreading behind the
> busiest line. Risograph texture, registration drift, no gradients, optimistic
> and legible.

> **Stop detail —** Extreme close crop of one stop on a risograph civic map: a
> circular node outlined in heavy ink, a small geometric glyph inside it, a
> fanned row of a dozen tiny tick-marks waiting beside it, a thin orange warning
> ring beginning to draw around it. Flat print texture, paper grain, no
> gradients.

> **Key art —** Poster for a game called PIRITORI → EDEN. Risograph-printed
> night city diagram of Helsinki's Kallio, heavy municipal type, one magenta
> line running through it toward a district at the very edge of the sheet that
> is cropped off by the paper's border. Charcoal, dirty off-white, cold blue,
> one orange accent. Screen-print flats, registration drift, grain. Bleak,
> dry, deadpan. No people, no crime-movie imagery, no neon glow.

---

## 7. The test

A finished image passes if a stranger can look at it and say **where the city
is failing** — which stop is over capacity, which line is overloaded — without
being told what any symbol means. If it is only atmospheric, it has missed.
