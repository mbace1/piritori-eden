Harju sports pitch (Brahenkenttä), Kallio — the `harju_pitch` site.

Made 2026-09-01, the third of the four originally-flagged placeholder sites
(jaska_studio already had art via scene-club, PR #11; staffed_bank got
bank-counter-v01, PR #17). Real public place, addressPrecision "public-anchor"
per the site record — fictional: false.

Built from an EXISTING, previously-unused reference photo found in
art-library/references/stages/stage-harju-sports-yard-night-v01.jpg — a real
top-down isometric render of the actual pitch (fenced yard, tiered concrete
stands, goal, basketball hoop, low brick clubhouse), attached as an image
reference and asked to match closely rather than described from scratch. Look
for the asset before making one (CLAUDE.md rule 5) — this one was already
sitting there, unregistered.

Two passes. The first reproduced the reference well but carried over the
reference's own painted graffiti lettering on the clubhouse wall ("HARJU
KALLIO") plus a small sign near the gate with near-readable marks on it — a
hard no per this project's baked_text rule. The second pass was a surgical
fix on the first generation rather than a fresh regeneration: blanked the
small sign, redrew the wall graffiti and fence tags as pure abstract
spray/scribble with no letterforms, kept everything else pixel-for-pixel.
Checked afterward by cropping every graffiti zone and the sign individually
and looking, not by a single glance at the full frame — the same discipline
that caught the bank scene's missing security mirror.

No people, no text anywhere, full-bleed. No live speaker registered for
enc-runner-at-tram-stop (no speaker_id on the encounter), so this is a plain
ambient location plate, not a STAGE_SPEC 6.3 counter scene — no empty gap for
a 3D presenter to composite into is needed here.
