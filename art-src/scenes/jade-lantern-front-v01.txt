Made 2026-09-01, the last of the four originally-flagged placeholder sites.
No existing source found for this one (checked first, per CLAUDE.md rule 5) -
unlike Harju, this had to be designed rather than found.

NARRATIVE.md is explicit and was read before writing any prompt: "The
fictional Jade Lantern Network... grows through restaurant fronts across
Kallio... Its members must be written as individuals rather than an ethnic
stereotype." The brief followed from that directly: an ordinary, dignified,
real-feeling small Kallio shopfront at closing time - one modest window,
condensation, a folding paper sign, ONE small plain lantern (not a decorative
string), delivery crates, a chained bicycle. Explicitly prompted AGAINST
dragon motifs, red-and-gold theming, strings of lanterns, pagoda shapes -
restrained and real, the same as any other shopfront on the street. The
encounter's own opening line ("Mei Lan serves the lunch queue before
acknowledging Aatami. The restaurant is a workplace first...") set the tone.

Four passes:
  v01 - first cut, on-theme and correctly restrained, but style-compared
        against bank-counter-v01 side by side and found noticeably smoother/
        more painterly than this repo's flat cut-cardstock register.
  v02 - restyle pass matching the flatter, coarser, grainier register.
        Genuinely closer on a second side-by-side comparison. Also carried
        the first real find: stencilled marks on the delivery crates reading
        as text/codes.
  v03 - asked to "remove the marks" on the crates directly; reduced but did
        not eliminate them (the model kept the label-shaped rectangle, just
        blurred its contents) - the SAME failure shape twice on the same
        object is exactly CLAUDE.md rule 8's stop condition for that one
        detail, so the next attempt changed approach rather than repeating it.
  v04 - replaced the crates with a plain unmarked wooden slat crate instead
        of asking to edit the marked ones. Worked immediately. Kept.

No live speaker is registered for enc-jade-window (no speaker_id on the
