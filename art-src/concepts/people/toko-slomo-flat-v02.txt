Style: hard black ink-line illustration over FLAT fills. Every garment is a small
number of broad, solid colour shapes with hard seams between them, like cut
paper. Line does the describing, not shading. NO gradients, NO airbrushed volume,
NO fabric wrinkles, NO cloth folds, NO creases, NO drop shadows, NO photographic
rendering, NO cloth simulation detail. A sleeve is one flat shape with an outline,
not a study of a sleeve. Keep the whole figure very simple and graphic.

Subject: a Helsinki noodle-stall cook in 2003, a man of about sixty, SHORT AND STOCKY
with a solid torso and slightly stooped shoulders. Arms and legs are ordinary
human proportion - arms reach only to mid-thigh when lowered, hands are normal
sized. Nothing is elongated or stretched. Grey hair, tanned forearms. He wears a plain
white shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, a plain BLACK apron over it,
plain black trousers and plain black shoes.

HIS FACE IS COMPLETELY HIDDEN BEHIND A RIGID GOLDEN-YELLOW MASK. The mask is a
smooth rounded shell, a broad egg shape wider than his head, in one flat saturated
golden yellow. Painted on its front in THICK, BOLD, PURE WHITE strokes, drawn like
a child's smiley and clearly visible from across a room:

  - one very wide upward-curving white grin, a fat white crescent, low on the mask
  - two upward-curving white arcs above it for closed happy eyes, each a thick
    white stroke

The white marks are BRIGHT WHITE against the gold with hard edges and high
contrast. They are not gold, not cream, not thin, not faint, not engraved and not
subtle. No nose, no mouth opening, no eye holes, no other features on the mask.
His grey hair shows above and to the sides of the mask. NONE of his real face is
visible - no skin, no eyes, no mouth, no chin. This mask is the character and it
must read instantly.

THE APRON IS COMPLETELY BLANK. No writing, no letters, no words, no Japanese
characters, no logo, no printed bowl, no picture, no patch, no pocket panel, no
badge, no embroidery, no decoration of any kind anywhere on it. It is one plain
solid black shape from top to bottom with a simple neck strap and a simple waist
tie.

He is in a strict T-POSE. Both arms are raised straight out sideways so they are
LEVEL WITH THE SHOULDERS and PARALLEL TO THE GROUND, forming one straight
horizontal line from the left fingertips through the shoulders to the right
fingertips. Palms down, fingers together. Legs straight and slightly apart, feet
flat and pointing forward. The shirt sleeves point straight out along the arms.
There is a clear open wedge of plain background visible in each armpit.

Strictly orthographic front elevation, facing the viewer square on. No
perspective, no foreshortening, no camera tilt, no lean, no twist.

The background is one completely flat, uniform light warm grey at a single value,
edge to edge and corner to corner, and NOTHING else is on it: no grain, no
speckle, no noise, no texture, no gradient, no vignette, no shadow, no cast
shadow, no contact shadow under the feet, no ground plane, no floor line, no
scenery, no border, no frame. Sampled anywhere it returns the same colour. The
figure has a clear margin of at least ten percent on all four sides.

There is no text anywhere in the image at all.

The image is exactly square, 1:1.
