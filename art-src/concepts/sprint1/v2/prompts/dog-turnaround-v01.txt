Style: hard black ink-line illustration with flat, slightly muted fills —
Darkest Dungeon rather than a comic — printed as a risograph: visible paper
grain over everything, screen-print colour separations, slight registration
drift between layers. Limited palette, hard seams between colours.

CONSTRUCTION, and this is the most important instruction in the prompt: build
the entire animal out of about six flat, solid shapes of a single uniform
colour each, as if cut from coloured paper with scissors and glued down. Every
colour meets its neighbour at a hard cut edge. A shape is one colour from edge
to edge — the same value at its centre as at its rim. The silhouette does all
the work. Read it as a paper cut-out of a dog, not as a drawing of a dog.

Subject: a medium-sized city mongrel, the kind that lives around a Helsinki
housing block in 2003. Deep chest, blunt muzzle, ragged ears — one up, one
half-folded — and a plain worn collar. Muddy brown and dirty cream, two flat
tones only. Alert but unimpressed. Coat length is described by the OUTLINE
being slightly rough and irregular, and by nothing else inside it.

Draw THREE views of this one same dog, laid out plainly in a single row,
evenly spaced, all at exactly the same scale, all standing on the same
invisible groundline, all the same height:
  left   — seen from directly the SIDE, in profile, facing the viewer's left,
           all four legs separated with background visible between them
  centre — seen from directly the FRONT, head on, forelegs apart
  right  — seen from directly BEHIND, tail down, hind legs apart, no face
Strictly orthographic: flat elevations, no perspective, no foreshortening, no
camera tilt, no vanishing point. Standing square on all four legs in all three.

The background is one completely flat, solid, uniform magenta, hex value
#FF00FF — pure full-intensity fuchsia, red channel 255, green channel 0, blue
channel 255. It is a harsh electric screen colour, NOT pink, NOT rose, NOT
dusty, NOT desaturated, NOT a muted printed pink. It runs edge to edge and
corner to corner behind and BETWEEN all three views at that one exact value,
with nothing on it: no gradient, no vignette, no shadow, no texture, no grain,
no border, no frame, no paper edge. No view touches the edge of the image.

No cast shadow, no contact shadow, no ground plane, no floor, no horizon, no
scenery. Nothing held. No circles or discs on the joints.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no hex codes, no
swatch strip, no colour chart, no watermark, no logo, no panel, no card, no
drop shadow, no mounted card. Not a presentation board — just the three views
on flat magenta.
