Style: the silhouette is built from a few broad pieces of cardstock, each keeping
a physical cut edge, small misalignment and visible overlap. Ink is reserved for
faces, joints, folds and one or two material cues. Marker strokes wobble, taper,
skip or slightly overshoot. Flat, slightly muted fills with visible paper grain.
No perfect vector contours, no airbrushed volume, no gradients, no glow, no tiny
costume rendering, no photographic shading. Helsinki in 2003, not a crime film
about it. Faces are specific caricatures with distinct noses, jaws and brows, and
carry humour, irritation, nerves or vanity as readily as threat.

Draw THREE DIFFERENT PEOPLE of the same type, laid out plainly in a single row,
evenly spaced, all at exactly the same scale, all standing on the same invisible
groundline, all the same height in frame.

EVERY figure is in a strict T-POSE. Both arms are raised straight out sideways so
they are LEVEL WITH THE SHOULDERS and PARALLEL TO THE GROUND, forming one
straight horizontal line from the left fingertips through the shoulders to the
right fingertips. Palms down, fingers together. Legs straight and slightly apart.
The arm span is as wide as the figure is tall.

THE SLEEVES GO WITH THE ARMS. Whatever coat, parka or jacket a figure wears, its
sleeves point straight out sideways along the raised arms. A heavy garment does
not hang down and does not hide the arms. On every figure there is a clear open
wedge of flat background visible in each armpit, between the underside of the arm
and the side of the body.

Arms hanging down at the sides is WRONG. Arms lowered, relaxed, folded, in
pockets, or angled downward is WRONG. This is a modelling reference, not a
portrait.

Strictly orthographic front elevation. No perspective, no foreshortening, no
camera tilt. Feet flat on an implied ground line. No cast shadow and no contact
shadow. Nothing held in either hand. No ground plane, no floor, no scenery.

The background is one completely flat, solid, uniform magenta, hex #FF00FF - pure
full-intensity fuchsia, red 255, green 0, blue 255. A harsh electric screen
colour, NOT pink, NOT rose, NOT dusty. It runs edge to edge and corner to corner
behind and BETWEEN all three figures at that one exact value, with nothing on it:
no gradient, no vignette, no shadow, no texture, no grain, no border, no frame.
No figure touches the edge of the image, and there is clear magenta between them.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no hex codes, no swatch
strip, no watermark, no logo, no panel, no card, no drop shadow, no mounted card.
Not a presentation board - just three figures on flat magenta.

Any tracksuit stripe is TWO stripes and there is no maker's mark, logo or brand
anywhere on any garment.

THE TYPE, and all three are this type:
HEAVY build - broad chest and shoulders, short negative spaces between arms and body. Doormen and enforcers who work with their hands. Heavy parka or padded work coat, work gloves or taped knuckles, sturdy boots. Thick necks, cropped or thinning hair, one with a beard. Vary age and ethnicity across the three.
