# Production direction — 2026-09-13

Status: owner production guidance. This is workflow/priority direction, not a GDD or Art Bible override.

## Governing principle

**Make progress.** Prefer concrete playable or visual improvement over process, branch, documentation or pipeline work that does not move the game forward.

## Current priority

The battle vertical has been the main blocker. The immediate proof should be **one visually convincing, playable battle environment** that demonstrates the fidelity and capability we want from the Dream Loop / GitHub-assisted pipeline.

- Environment/look first: turn useful 2D concept direction into a convincing 3D space.
- The 3D result may reinterpret the concept substantially — voxel, stylised, realistic or otherwise — if it looks good, makes sense for Eden and moves production forward.
- Add enough battle logic to make the scene genuinely playable and to expose what the next design/technical problems are.
- Do not wait for final characters to prove the environment and interaction loop.

## Characters and assets

Current 3D characters are not the visual bar: rigging and animation remain visibly janky. Use honest placeholders/stand-ins until a character actually clears the quality gate.

Add production assets when they fit the vertical, rather than filling the project with assets first and trying to justify them later.

Concept art is a lead and a target for interpretation, not a requirement for literal 1:1 reconstruction.

## Parallel work

Parallel tracks are welcome when each has a concrete useful output. Current useful tracks include:

- battle: one polished, readable, playable fight;
- environment/3D: one strong concept-to-3D Dream Loop proof;
- character pipeline: prove one character cleanly, then repeat once before scaling;
- narrative/economy: one complete action → consequence loop;
- map/chapter: one functioning neighbourhood/run loop.

Battle and environment are the current leading tracks because solving them unlocks the rest.

## Arena experiments

Bear Path and related C-series arena work are experiments and production tests. They can teach camera, readability, occlusion, materials, lighting, capacity and pipeline lessons. They do **not** override the GDD, Art Bible or the authored Era I vertical-slice structure.

## Agent behaviour

Agents should default to making progress and reporting concrete results. Escalate genuine design conflicts to the owner; do not repeatedly ask the owner to reconstruct repository state or manage routine handoffs.
